Fourth State Applications Research Laboratory

The [Fourth State Applications Research (4-STAR) Laboratory](https://ne.ncsu.edu/4-star/) in the
[North Carolina State University Department of Nuclear Engineering](https://ne.ncsu.edu/) studies
the controlled formation of the plasma state using electromagnetic energy and how plasma can
contribute to advanced manufacturing in a wide range of applications spanning electronics, energy,
medicine, and agriculture.  We combine source design, novel methods for plasma measurement, and
[computer simulation](https://github.com/shannon-lab) to advance plasma science in these critical
areas of the U.S. economy.

The 4-STAR Lab is led by [Dr. Steven Shannon](https://ne.ncsu.edu/people/scshanno).

## Zapdos

!style halign=left
The Zapdos code is developed by the 4-STAR Lab working in conjunction with the University of Illinois
at Urbana-Champaign, Idaho National Laboratory, and others. Zapdos, a [MOOSE-based](https://mooseframework.inl.gov)
application, focuses on plasma physics modeling capabilities using a fluid description for multiphysics
applications. Currently, Zapdos has been applied to:

- [plasma-liquid interactions](https://doi.org/10.1088/0022-3727/49/23/235204),
- [radio-frequency capacitively-coupled discharges](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2023.108837), and
- the plasma edge (e.g., the scrape-off layer) of fusion devices

amongst many others!

Zapdos is open-source and available on GitHub at [https://github.com/shannon-lab/zapdos](https://github.com/shannon-lab/zapdos).
The documentation website can be found at [https://shannon-lab.github.io/zapdos](https://shannon-lab.github.io/zapdos).
